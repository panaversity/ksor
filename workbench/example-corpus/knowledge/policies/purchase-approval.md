# Purchase approval thresholds

Purchases are approved according to total contract value:

| Total value      | Required approval       |
| ---------------- | ----------------------- |
| Up to $5,000     | Team lead               |
| $5,001 – $50,000 | Department head         |
| Over $50,000     | Chief Financial Officer |

Split purchases designed to stay under a threshold are treated as a single
purchase at their combined value.

This policy does not address purchases made in foreign currencies. A question
about currency conversion is **outside this corpus** — the correct behavior for
an agent is to say so, not to improvise. (That sentence is load-bearing: the
first abstention eval targets it.)
