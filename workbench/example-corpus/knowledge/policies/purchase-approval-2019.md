# Purchase approval thresholds (2019 policy)

This policy was replaced by
[Purchase approval thresholds](./purchase-approval.md) and is retained because
superseded documents are marked, never deleted — an agent citing the 2019
thresholds must be able to discover that they no longer govern.

Historical thresholds: purchases over $25,000 required CFO approval; there was
no split-purchase clause.
