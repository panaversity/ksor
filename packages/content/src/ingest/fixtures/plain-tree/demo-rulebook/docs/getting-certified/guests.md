# Rule 11: Guests and Minors

Guests may observe from the marked walkway but may never operate a machine, badge or no badge.
A member may sponsor at most two guests at a time and remains responsible for their conduct.
Minors aged fourteen to seventeen may earn badges for the laser cutter and the 3D printers only,
and must have a certified parent or guardian physically present in the same room while operating
anything. No one under fourteen passes the yellow line, full stop.
