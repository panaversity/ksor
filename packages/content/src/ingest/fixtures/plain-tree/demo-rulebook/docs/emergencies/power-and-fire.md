# Rule 31: Power Cuts and Fire

On a power cut, every machine must be switched to OFF at its own switch before the master
breaker is reset — machines that restart on their own when power returns have taken fingers.
On a fire alarm, shut your machine if it is one motion, otherwise leave it; the muster point is
the far corner of the parking lot by the oak tree. Members do not fight fires larger than a
waste bin. Stewards check the bays; nobody re-enters until the fire service says so.
