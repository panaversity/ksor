# Rule 10: Machine Badges

A machine badge is earned, not granted. To earn a badge for any powered machine you must (a)
attend the machine's orientation, (b) demonstrate the startup and shutdown sequence to a steward
without prompting, and (c) name the machine's two most likely failure modes and the correct
response to each. Badges are stored on your member card and checked by the door reader at the
machine bay. A badge lapses after twelve months without a logged session on that machine; a
lapsed badge requires a refresher demonstration, not the full orientation.
