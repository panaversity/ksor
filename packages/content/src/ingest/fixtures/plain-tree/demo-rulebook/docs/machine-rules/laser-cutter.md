# Rule 21: The Laser Cutter

Approved materials only — the laminated list on the lid is the whole law. PVC and vinyl are
banned absolutely: burning them releases chlorine gas that corrodes the optics and your lungs in
the same afternoon. Never leave a running job unattended; the operator stays within arm's reach
of the lid until the job completes. The fume extractor must show green airflow before the beam
fires. Small fires happen: open the lid, smother with the fire blanket, and log the incident —
the CO2 extinguisher is for fires the blanket cannot kill.
